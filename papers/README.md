# BrainStem papers

Preprint drafts about individual functions of BrainStem and the theories behind them. Each folder holds the paper (`paper.md`), its references (`references.bib`), its figures and the data and script that rebuild them. All drafts are unreviewed and claim no more than the measured results support.

| Folder | Paper | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `P1_learning_clock_reproducibility` | Learning on an Experience Clock: A General Method for Repeatable and Paired Evaluation of Stochastic Learners, Demonstrated on a Biologically Inspired Text Learner | draft of 17 November 2026 |

Rebuild the figures of a paper with `python figures/make_figures.py` inside its folder (Python standard library only).
